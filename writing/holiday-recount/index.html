The holiday

Six days at a beach house. One tent. One kayak trip. One family that apparently could not be trusted with either.

A family goes away for a relaxing holiday. By the end, the tent has escaped, a kayak is in a bush, and Dad has banned outdoor equipment.
Day 1The tent is put up. The wind has other plans.
Day 2Kayaking goes badly for reasons that were, technically, avoidable.
