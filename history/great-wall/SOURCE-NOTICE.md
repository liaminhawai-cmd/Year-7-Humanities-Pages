# Source notice: the 3.2 wall, the Great Wall

`bump-it-up.html` is the bump-it-up wall for Ancient China lesson 3.2, the
Great Wall. Linked from the China page's Lessons section (in
`tools/build_index_pages.py`). `bump-it-up-standalone.html` is the one-file copy
(`py tools/build_standalone.py history/great-wall/bump-it-up.html`). The engine is
a copy of `history/terracotta/bump-it-up.html` with its own password.

`content.js` is GENERATED by `tools/gen_great_wall_wall.js`. Edit the generator
and rerun it. It copies rows 1 and 5 and their curriculum codes from
`history/fu-hao/content.js`, and the interpretations row and its S06 codes from
`history/china/content.js`, all word for word.

## Why these sources

The wall practises historical interpretations, rubric row 4. The question is
"Was the Great Wall a success?". Source A is a photo; Source B is a historian's
interpretation as Wikipedia reports it; Source C is a video's own words. B and C
disagree about whether there was ever one Great Wall. None of the three is in an
assessment. The printed sheet (`print/China-3.2-*`) has the same sources on its back.

Interpretations has its own colour on this wall (magenta, ▼, dotted), because the
purple ◆ that the China wall uses for it is already "accuracy, usefulness and
reliability" here.
