# Source notice: the Fu Hao owl wall

`bump-it-up.html` is the bump-it-up wall for Ancient China lesson 2.2, Social
groups. Linked from the China page's Lessons section (in
`tools/build_index_pages.py`). `bump-it-up-standalone.html` is the one-file copy,
made by `py tools/build_standalone.py history/fu-hao/bump-it-up.html`.

## Why this vessel

The 2.2 lesson's source is an ox-shaped bronze zun. No record of that exact
vessel could be checked, so the wall uses the Fu Hao owl zun instead: the same
kind of object (an animal-shaped bronze wine vessel) with a secure, checkable
record. It is in no assessment. It is like the bronze bell of Task 1 in the
Source Analysis Booklet, so the thinking transfers and the answers do not.

## The photo

`fu-hao-owl-zun.jpg` is Wikimedia Commons "Shang Bronze Owl Zun - side view.jpg"
by Gary Todd, released CC0 (public domain dedication), resized to 960 x 1440.
National Museum of China. The hotspot boxes in `content.js` are percentages of
this file; check them again if it is replaced.
