# Source notice: the 3.1 wall, the kneeling archer

`bump-it-up.html` is the bump-it-up wall for Ancient China lesson 3.1, the
Terracotta Army. Linked from the China page's Lessons section (in
`tools/build_index_pages.py`). `bump-it-up-standalone.html` is the one-file copy
(`py tools/build_standalone.py history/terracotta/bump-it-up.html`). The engine is a
copy of `history/fu-hao/bump-it-up.html` with its own password, and one change: a
locked rung with no rubric wording for a row shows that row's plain line instead.

`content.js` is GENERATED by `tools/gen_terracotta_wall.js`. Edit the generator
and rerun it. It copies rows 1 and 5 and the curriculum codes from
`history/fu-hao/content.js`, and the perspectives row from `history/china/content.js`,
all word for word.

## Why this source

It practises historical perspectives, rubric row 3, which no other wall models:
the emperor's point of view and the workers'. It is in no assessment. Booklet
Task 3 on Qin Shi Huang uses a painting of the book burning and two historians,
not the army. The printed sheet (`print/China-3.1-*`) has the same source on its back.

## The picture

`kneeling-archer.jpg` is Wikimedia Commons "Qin State Terracotta Kneeling Archer,
Painted (9932580424).jpg" by Gary Todd, CC0, photographed in 2009 at the Shaanxi
Provincial Museum's Qin gallery. Resized to 1280 x 1920. The hotspot boxes in the
generator are percentages of this file.

## Levels 7 to 10

The rubric rows show for every level from the start. The worked examples of
Levels 7 to 10 are hidden until the teacher types "mausoleum" on the page. The
examples are still in `content.js`, so a student who opens the file can read them.
