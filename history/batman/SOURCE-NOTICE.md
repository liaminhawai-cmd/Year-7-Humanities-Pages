# Source and cultural-use notice

## The painting

`batman-treaty-source.jpg` is *Batman's Treaty with the Aborigines at Merri
Creek*, John Wesley Burtt, oil on canvas, circa 1875. State Library of Victoria,
Pictures Collection, accession H92.196. It is reproduced here at 1600x800.

The painting is long out of copyright. What has **not** been confirmed is the
State Library's own terms for this reproduction of it. That is a question about
the photograph, not the painting, and it needs answering before this image is
used in print or anywhere commercial.

## The Proclamation

Proclamation of Governor Sir Richard Bourke, 26 August 1835, National Archives of
Australia. Reproduced as text, abridged, with spelling and capitalisation as
written. An Australian government document of 1835 is in the public domain.

## Open consultation item

**The framing of this wall has not been checked with the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung
Cultural Heritage Aboriginal Corporation.**

This matters because of what the sources are. Both were made by colonists: a
painting by an artist who was not there, and a Proclamation written by the
Governor. No Kulin record of the meeting at Merri Creek has survived, and the
wall says so, in the facts panel and in the worked examples. But saying it is not
the same as having the framing reviewed by the people whose Country and ancestors
the sources are about.

The wall is published because the unit is being taught and students need the
resource. The item above is open, is named on the topic page rather than only
here, and closing it may change the wording.

## Naming

The people at the meeting are named in the sources as a group and not as
individuals. Where this resource says "Kulin", that is the language grouping the
sources and the State Library record use. Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung people are the
Traditional Owners of the land where the meeting took place.
