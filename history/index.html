The fullest resource set on the site: a wall, level sheets, a paragraph builder and the print pack. an object rather than a document.🏺Ancient Egypt archived: Herodotus on the Nile, a visitor writing much of it at second hand. Built as History 2 before the unit list was checked; Year 7 does not teach an Egypt unit. Kept because it is a complete second-hand-report source and works as extra practice.

The three units are not assessed on the same skill. CAT 1: Ancient Australia is a source-analysis test: primary and secondary sources, reliability and accuracy, and First Nations beliefs, values and lives. The wall on that page rehearses it, and does it with two sources that disagree, which is what comparing interpretations needs. CAT 2: Ancient Rome is a tourist guidebook for a traveller arriving in 300 CE, and assesses no source analysis at all. So the Rome page holds a Roman source read at every level: a source for the unit, not a model of its task. CAT 3: Ancient China is two booklets, chronology skills and source analysis, and adds historical perspectives and historical significance. Its wall is that booklet's own rubric, level for level.

The booklet and the word list are the same for every topic, so they sit on each topic page rather than here.
