What each unit assesses is not the same. Ancient Australia and Ancient China are assessed on source analysis; Ancient China adds historical perspectives and significance. The Ancient Rome CAT is a tourist guidebook marked on chronology, cause and effect, metacognition and presentation — not on source analysis. The Vesuvius wall is a source for that unit, not its assessment task.
