# Source notice: the 2.3 Daily life module

`module.html` is Ancient China lesson 2.3 as a full-screen module for a
work-from-home day. Hidden: not in `tools/build_index_pages.py`, noindex, opened
by its address.

## Source 1: Michael Loewe

A short extract (about 70 words) from Michael Loewe, *Everyday Life in Early
Imperial China* (1968), quoted exactly as it appears in the 2.3 OneNote lesson,
which spells the name "Lowe". The book is in copyright; the extract is a short
quotation inside original teaching material, attributed, on a hidden page. The
same extract is Task 1, Source 2 of the Source Analysis Booklet; the teacher
chose to use it here knowing that.

## Source 2: the Shang chariot burial

`shang-chariot-burial.jpg` is Wikimedia Commons "Shang Chariot Sacrificial Pit
01.jpg" by Gary Todd, released CC0 (public domain dedication), resized to 960
wide. Yin Ruins (Yinxu), Anyang. The photographer's own description says the
chariots are genuine and the skeletons on show are copies; the module says so
in the caption and uses it as a Level 9 checklist point.
