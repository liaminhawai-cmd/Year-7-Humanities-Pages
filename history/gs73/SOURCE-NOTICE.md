## Historical interpretations used in Poster 6

The dating comparison summarises two published archaeological positions. It does not imply disagreement about whether GS73 is a genuine grinding-stone artefact.

- Clarkson, C. et al. (2017). “Human occupation of northern Australia by 65,000 years ago.” *Nature*, 547, 306–310. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature22968
- Williams, M. A. J., Spooner, N. A., McDonnell, K., & O’Connell, J. F. (2021). “Identifying disturbance in archaeological sites in tropical northern Australia: Implications for previously proposed 65,000-year continental occupation date.” *Geoarchaeology*, 36(1), 92–108. https://doi.org/10.1002/gea.21822

The source record also uses results reported for GS73 in Hayes et al. (2022): morphology, usewear, 41 recovered starch grains, plant-related chemical compounds and identified plastic contamination. The initial source display withholds the researchers’ functional conclusion so students can infer from the recorded evidence before revealing it.
